## Improved Positive Bias Temperature Instability of  n-type Vertical C-Shaped-Channel Nanosheet FET  by Forming Gas Annealing   

Yunfei Shi 1,3 , Songyi Jiang 1,3 , Hong Yang 1,3\* , Yongkui Zhang 1,3\* , Longda Zhou 2 , Zhigang Ji 2 , Qianqian Liu 1, , Qi Wang 1 , Huilong Zhu 1,3 ,  Jun Luo 1,3  and Wenwu Wang 1   1  Key Laboratory of Microelectronics Device & Integrated Technology, Institute of Microelectronics, Chinese Academy of Science, Beijing  100029, China  2  National Key Laboratory of Science and Technology on Micro/Nano Fabrication, School of Microelectronics, Shanghai Jiaotong  University, Shanghai 200240, China  3  Microelectronics Institute, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing 100049, China  Phone : (86)10-82995916, Email:  yanghong@ime.ac.cn, zhangyongkui@ime.ac.cn

Abstract — In this article, the influence of Forming Gas  Annealing (FGA) on the Positive Bias Temperature Instability  (PBTI)   characteristics   of   n-vertical   C-shaped-channel  nanosheet FET (n-VCNFET) is studied. The experimental  results show that the extra FGA can significantly suppress both  the initial and generated interface traps in PBTI. Moreover, in  ultra-fast PBTI the pre-existing trap and total trap of VCNFET  due to FGA decreases by   $\mathbf{35\%}$   and   $\bf{31\%}$  , respectively. The  energy level of the oxide trap under PBTI and recovery doesn’t  change, in other words, the FGA induces the oxide trap density  of the devices to decrease by  $36\%$   at   $\bf{125^{\circ}C}$   and 1.4V V OV . The  optimization effect of FGA annealing has been further  confirmed from the perspective of trap generation. It provides  a guideline for the PBTI improvement of VCNFET in trap  scopes.

Keywords—reliability, Positive Bias Temperature Instability  (PBTI), nanosheet (NS), Vertical C-Shaped-Channel Nanosheet  FET (VCNFET), forming gas annealing (FGA), trap generation

## I.    I NTRODUCTION  

Gate-All-Around Field Effect Transistor (GAAFET) has  already been considered as the replacement for FinFETs due  to its better gate control [1-3]. Compared to the horizontal  GAA, the vertical GAA offers advantages in terms of easier  gate length control and integration within a limited area [4],  making it a prime candidate for future integrated circuits. In  the development from FinFET to GAA, the device structure  has gradually become more complex, and Positive Bias  Temperature Instability (PBTI) has always been a concern [5- 8] because PBTI reliability is a long-term problem [9]. The  Vertical C-Shaped-Channel Nanosheet FET (VCNFET) in  this study is a type of vertical GAA. For VCNFET, which has  a more complex structure and process, it is necessary to study  the changes in PBTI reliability and optimize the process.

In our previous work, the BTI reliability of VCNFETs was  simply compared, but not deeply studied [10]. In this work,  we investigate optimizing PBTI reliability in n-VCNFETs by  forming gas annealing (FGA). Through analyzing the basic  electrical characteristics of the device and using the measurestress-measure (MSM) method, and ultrafast measure-stressmeasure (UF-MSM) method, we verified the optimization of  PBTI reliability in n-VCNFETs by FGA annealing. We  conducted an in-depth analysis of the optimization from a  trap-based view through the discharge-based multi-pulse  energy Profiling (DMP) techniques and Secondary Ion Mass  Spectroscopy (SIMS). The results provide a practical  guideline for the optimization of VCNFET fabrication  processes.

## IV.   C ONCLUSION 

We experimentally investigated the electrical and PBTI  characteristics of n-VCNFETs by FGA and analyzed details  from the perspective of traps. The initial smaller SS and PBTI  improvement of n-VCNFETs with FGA is due to the fewer  pre-existing and generated interface traps in n-VCNFETs. At  the same time, the generated oxide trap density of n

VCNFETs is efficiently reduced by   $31\%$   due to FGA under  PBTI stress with 1.4V V OV . The results are valuable for the  process optimization of VCNFETs.
