## Failure Analysis of a Bus Circuit under Slow  Power-on Induced by Process Deviation  

Shuo Wang 2 , Dongmei   $\mathrm{Li}^{3}$  , Hao  $\mathrm{{H}\mathrm{{u}^{1}}}$  , Tingyao Zhang 4 , Shijian  $\mathrm{Li^{4}}$  , Xinzhong Zhu 4 , Yiming   $\mathrm{{Nu}^{1}}$  , Yi Zhao 1 , Pengfei Lian 1\*    1   School of Integrated Circuits, East China Normal University, No. 500 Dongchuan Road, Minhang District, Shanghai 200241, China  2  No. 808 Institute of Shanghai Academy of Spaceflight Technology, 3888 Yuanjiang Road, Minhang District, Shanghai, China  3   Beijing Microelectronics Technology Institute, No.2 Siyingmen North Road, Donggaodi, Fengtai District, Beijingi, China    4   Shanghai Institute of Satellite Engineering, Shanghai Academy of Spaceflight Technology, 3666 Yuanjiang Road, Minhang District,  Shanghai, China

Email: lianpf@mail.ustc.edu.cn

Abstract —Failure analysis of a bus circuit under slow  power-on induced by process deviation. A bus circuit fails with  slow power-on at low temperature. The failure reason of the  bus circuit is the reference source of the transmitter is invalid,  which is resulted in the offset of the amplifier of the reference  source. The failure mechanism of the bus circuit is analyzed.  The offset of the amplifier of the reference source is resulted in  the process deviation of the reference source. If the power-on  time of the bus circuit is long, the diode of the triode of the  reference source is not open, resulting in failure of the reference  source. To avoid this failure, the increased test with longer  power-on time is proposed.

Keywords—Bus Circuit, failure, Slow Power-on, Process  Deviation

## I.    I NTRODUCTION  

MIL-STD-1553B, designed by the United States  Department of Defense, establishes the mechanical,  electrical, and functional characteristics of a serial data bus  [1]. The latest version of the serial Local Area Network  (LAN) for military avionics, was released in 1978. Despite  having been in use for over 30 years, the data bus has  consistently demonstrated reliability and remains the most  widely adopted militarized network. The 1553B bus network  utilizes a linear topology, connecting a bus controller (BC)  and various remote terminals (RT). To enhance fault  tolerance, a dual-channel design is employed [2].

The 1553B bus circuit is extensively utilized worldwide  in the fields of aviation and space, with a notable presence in  manned spaceflight. Initially implemented on the F-16  Falcon fighter aircraft, its adoption swiftly extended to other  aircraft designs such as the F-18 Hornet, AH-64 Apache, P3C Orion, F-15 Eagle, and F-20 Tiger Shark. This  standardized communication protocol is widely embraced by  all branches of the U.S. military and is a preferred choice for  NASA [3].

Due to the widespread application of the 1553B bus in  high-value aerospace vehicles and the difficulty of timely  repairs at remote terminals, research on its reliability has  become particularly crucial. In previous studies, Zhang et al.  focused on investigating how to handle RAM faults in the  1553B bus circuit during the in-orbit operation of spacecraft  [4]. On the other hand, He et al. concentrated on improving  the   reliability   of   the   1553B   bus   circuit   through  communication software processing methods [5]. Although  these studies mainly centered around enhancing the  reliability of the 1553B bus circuit through design  approaches, there is a scarcity of reports on electrical stress  failures caused by processes during usage. This indicates the  ongoing need for in-depth research and resolution of  potential electrical stress failure issues in practical

applications to further enhance the reliability of the 1553B  bus circuit. This paper focus on failure analysis of a bus  circuit under slow power-on induced by process deviation,  and has a practical value to improve the similar reliability of  the bus circuit.

If there is a large inverted offset in the amplifier of the  reference source generation circuit, the reference source of  the transmitter will not in the normal working area and the  limited amplitude output of the triple reference source buffer  generation circuit will decrease, leading to the invalid of the  transmitter. The input offset of the amplifier is resulted in the  size and the threshold voltage of the input differential pair,  the process variation of the gain and the junction voltage of  the triodes. The worst condition is when all the factors  accumulate, resulting in the offset of the amplifier of the  reference source of the transmitter. In a word, the failure  reason of the bus circuit is the increased offset of the  amplifier of the reference source of the transmitter, which is  resulted in the deviation of process parameters of the  reference source of the transmitter.

## V.   F AILURE MECHANISM ANALYSIS 

The bus circuit fails during slow power-on due to the  increased offset voltage resulting from the process deviation  of the transmitter's reference source. The base-emitter  junction voltage of the diodes of the triodes of the reference  source of the transmitter is inversely proportional with the  working temperature. The lower of the working temperature,  the smaller the junction voltage of the diode of the triode is.  Therefore, the bus circuit may fail in the low working  temperature. On the other hand, the failure of the bus circuit  with big offset is related to the power-on time. Bigger offset  and longer power-on time of the bus circuit lead to  a greater probability of failure. In long power-on time, the  base-emitter junction voltage of the diode of the triode of the  reference source increased slowly because of the capacitance  characteristic. Therefore, the diode cannot open, resulting in  abnormal working of the reference source. The two areas are decided by the offset voltage and the  power-on time. If the offset is big, the bus circuit is failure in  a long power-on time and is normal in a short power-on time.  By the increased test and the application verification, a safe  working area can be generated, which can work normally in  any conditions. In the increased test, the power-on time of  the bus circuit is longer than the normal power-on time when  working. What’s more, the power-on curve is smooth and the  working voltage of the power supply is decreased in the  increased test.

## VII.   C ONCLUSION 

A failure mechanism of the bus circuit under slow  power-on is analyzed. The failure reason of the bus circuit is  the offset of the reference source caused by the process  deviation. The failure phenomenon gets worse with the  decrease of the working temperature. Because of the  combined action of the offset and the power-on time of the  bus circuit, the bus circuit fails. An increased test with longer  power-on time is effective to solve the failure of the bus  circuit.
