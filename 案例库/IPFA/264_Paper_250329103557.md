## Failure analysis of ASIC controller integrated in embedded flash memory package under biasedHAST reliabilityfailure 

Yong Yang Gan', Bee Chin Loke', Aik Leng Tan', May Shin Chang', Nirbhaya Pathak 1 Department of Failure Analysis Engineering, Western Digital (SanDisk Storage Malaysia Sdn. Bhd.), Plot 301A, Persiaran Cassia Selatan 1, Batu Kawan, Penang Phone  (60)179053161,Email  yong.yang.gan@wdc.com

Abstract—The emergence of embedded flashmemory specifically for 3D NAND technology represents a significant advancementindatastorageforelectronicdevices.This innovativeapproachoffersacompellingcombinationof compactness,improved operational efficiency, and superior performanceacrossdiverseapplications.However, understanding and mitigating potential failure mechanisms is critical toensuredevice reliabilityandpreventfuture malfunctions.This studyinvestigates the characterization of electricaloverstress (EOS)within the application-specific integrated circuit(ASIC) controllerforembeddedflashmemory utilizing 3D NAND technology. The investigation employs a biased high accelerated stress test (Biased-HAST) with a controlledvariationofbiasconditionsandstressdurationsto study ontheproductreliability.The characterization of defect within the ASIC controller using Biased-HAST provides valuable insights for improving device reliability and ensuring long-term functionality.Byunderstanding these failure mechanisms, manufacturers can implement preventative measures and extend the lifespan of these advanced storage devices.

Keywords-3D  NAND,ASIC  controller,  lock-in thermography(LIT),Faultisolation,electricaloverstress(EOS)

## 1.INTRODUCTION 

Embeddedflashmemoryplays a critical rolein enabling variouselectronicdevicestostoredataefficiently.By integrating the Application-Specific Integrated Circuit (ASIC) controller and 3D NAND memory dies on a single package,embeddedflashmemorymaintains a compactsize, ideal for space-constrained applications. These devices are designed toworkseamlessly with3DNAND,enablingfaster data access and transfer speeds compared to using separate components[l].Thistranslatestoquickerapplaunches, smoother data processing, and an overall enhanced user experienceinvariouselectronicdevices.

Reliabilitytestisessentialforembeddedflashmemoryto ensure it meets theperformance and longevityexpectations ofspecificapplications[2].Reliabilitytestofteninvolves pushing the memorybeyond its typical operating conditions, which able toidentify weakness and potential failure modes ofthememory.Embeddedflashmemoryisacrucial componentinawiderangeofdevices,frommobiledevices toautomotives.Itsreliability directlyimpactsthe functionality and safety of thesedevices.Biased highly acceleratedstresstest(Biased-HAST)iscommonlyusedfor flash memory package by applying a constant voltage that is

higher than the typical operating voltage, as well as higher temperature and humidity. This accelerates the degradation mechanisms that occur during normal operation conditions, allowingidentification of devicemuchfaster.

Understandingthereliabilityfailuresiscrucialfor improving device reliabilityand preventing future occurrences.Toachievethis,failureanalysisfor embedded flashmemoryafterreliabilityisessentialtoidentifythe defect location and defect mode. Electrical overstress (EOS) poses a significant threat to the functionality and lifespan of memorycontrollers.Biased-HASTreliabilityundervarious condition was carriedout to evaluate onASICcontroller reliabilityperformance.

## II.METHODOLOGY 

Embeddedflashmemorypackageconsistsof 3DNAND memoryandASICcontrollerdieswereusedforthisstudy. After assembly, system test was conducted to ensure all the units are electrically and functionally passed. Next, the packagesunderwenttoBHASTreliabilitytestingfor264 hoursat  $110^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$   $85\%$  relative humidity. The power pins were supplied with 3.6V,1.3V,and 1.95Vfor pins1,2,and 3, respectively.By subjecting thememory tohigh temperatures, voltages, and read/write cycles,BHAST accelerates the development of failures that might take much longer to occur undernormal use.

ApowerdipduringBiased-HASTchamberoperation causedthechambertostopat57and189hours.This prevented the reliability system from downloading firmware, haltingBiased-HAST.Multipleunitswerefound tohavehigh current supply on the power pin,leading to the firmware download failure.These units were identified for further investigation through failure analysis. The remaining unit continued and successfully completed the 264-hour BiasedHAST reliability test. Following the reliability test, the current of all power pins was measured, and then the units underwent system testing.

To identify the failure mode, non-destructive analyses were performed, including optical inspection, 2D X-ray, confocal scanning acoustic microscopy (CSAM), curve tracing,and 3DNANDfunctional testing with a dedicated tester. Subsequently, lock-in thermography (LIT) from the Thermo Fisher Scientific ELITE system was employed to localize the defect at the package level [3]. After non

destructiveanalyses,theunitsunderwentdieisolationand chemical decapsulation for subsequent characterization analysis including scanning electron microscope (SEM) and energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDX). Further analysis including the grain analysis and focused ion beam (FIB) cross section were carried out to analyze on the defect mechanism.

During EOS, a rapid escalation in temperature can induce melting of gold, copper, and aluminum due to their inherent thermal properties. The subsequentsolidificationofmoltenmetalalloyleadstothe formation of dendrite microstructure [7]. Gold and copper will first solidify due to high solidification temperature as compared to aluminum. The solidification process is exothermic,signifying the release of latent heat that maintains a constant temperature for gold and copper until complete solidification of the liquid phase[8]. However, this phenomenon creates a temperature difference at the solidliquidinterfacebetween thegold/copper and aluminum phases. The liquid phase experiences a more rapid temperaturedecreasecomparedtothesolid-liquidinterface. Constitutional undercooling plays a role in determining the final microstructure of the solidified material, which can impact its mechanical properties. At this interface, a region of constitutional undercooling may be present under extreme heat flux conditions. If a random protuberance develops on the interface, its tip will reside within the liquid phase, which will experience a strong driving force for solidification due to the lower surrounding temperature. This phenomenon can also lead to the formation of secondary dendrite arms, ultimately resulting in a dendritic interface.

## IV. CONCLUSION 

EOS in embedded flash memory arises from a complex interplay of factors throughout the entire manufacturing lifecycle, including device design, wafer processing. assembly, testing, and operating conditions. Replacing the controller wafer with a new batch resulted in all units passing Biased-HAST reliability testing. The root cause of ASIC controller failures can be attributed to two primary categories of triggering factors. The first category encompasses latent waferor assemblydefects,suchas delamination,microcrack, metal-metal bridging, and metal distortion. These defects are inherently difficult to detect during standard electrical testing due to their subtle nature. The second category involves operational stress events, exemplified by power dips. During a power dip,the device under test (DUT)experiences a sudden changein current as it attemptstomaintain operation. This rapid current surge can lead to excessive Joule heating within the controller die,exceeding its thermal designlimits and potentially resulting in EOs failure. In short, to ensure robust embedded flash memory packaging performance,

addressing potential weaknesses across all production stages, and incorporating stress mitigation strategies during device designtohandlereal-world operating condition approach is necessary.
