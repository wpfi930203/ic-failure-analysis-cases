##  Power Amplifier Die Moisture Ingress Point  Detection Using Nano-Fluorescent Dye Penetration  Method  

Ewe Chhong Tan 1 , Jinzhi Lois Liao 2  1 Broadcom (Malaysia), Bayan Lepas Free Industrial Zone, 11900 Bayan Lepas,Penang Malaysia    2 Wintech Nano-Technology Services Pte Ltd, The Alpha #03-26, 10 Science Park Rd, Singapore Science Park II, Singapore   Phone: +604 6108363 \*Email: ewe-chhong.tan@broadcom.com  +65 67777354 \*Email: lois@wintech-nano.com

Abstract — In wafer fabrication, silicon nitride (Si 3 N 4 ) layer  is widely used in semiconductor devices as a passivation layer to  prevent moisture ingress into the die that would cause problems  like delamination, corrosion and metal migration, etc. In this  study, a Power Amplifier (PA) die which is passivated with a  layer of Si3N4 was found to have delamination, corrosion, and  metal migration after uHAST 96hrs that caused Icc leakage  failure. It is suspected that the failures were caused by moisture  invasion. However, traditional FA techniques such as Scanning  Acoustic Microscopy (C-SAM) and X-Ray inspection cannot  detect any defect of the die due to their detection limit.

To solve the above challenges, Nano-Fluorescent dye  penetration test was performed to determine the possible  moisture ingress point that resulted in the delamination,  corrosion, and metal corrosion in the PA die. After florescent  liquid soaking, the die was inspected under fluorescent  microscope. Sign of fluorescent residues were observed on the  PA die which could be the possible moisture ingress point.  Focused Ion Beam (FIB) cross sections were performed to verify  the fluorescent finding. It is found that Si 3 N 4  crack and  Benzocyclobutene (BCB) delamination under the fluorescent  residues that allowed the moisture ingress into the die and  caused the delamination, corrosion and metal migration that  resulted in the Icc leakage failure. This work shows that NanoFluorescent dye method is a useful and yet economical FA  technique to detect surface defect of the passivation layer of a  die.

Keywords—Passivation Layer, Si 3 N 4 , uHAST, delamination,  Fluorescence Test

## I.    I NTRODUCTION 

A Power Amplifier (PA) die encountered leakage failure  after reliability test uHAST (Unbiased Highly Accelerated  Stress Test) 96hrs. It is speculated that during the uHAST, the  moisture invaded into the die and accumulated at the failed  area and subsequently caused the leakage. Although it is  suspected that the failure was caused by moisture invasion,  traditional FA techniques such as Scanning Acoustic  Microscopy (C-SAM) and X-Ray Inspection cannot detect  any defect of the die due to their detection limit.

To solve above issues, Nano-Fluorescent dye penetration  test was performed to determine the possible moisture ingress  point that resulted in the delamination, corrosion and metal  migration in the PA die. The testing die was soaked into  fluorescent dye for 24 hours. If there is any surface defect  exist on the passivation layer, the fluorescence liquid will  flow into the crack. Subsequently, the die was examined

under fluorescence microscope after discharging from the  fluorescence liquid.

Sign of fluorescent residue was observed on the PA die  which could be the possible moisture ingress point. Focused  Ion Beam (FIB) cross sections were performed to verify the  fluorescent finding. It is found that Si 3 N 4  crack and  Benzocyclobutene (BCB) delamination highlighted with the  presence of fluorescent residues suggest the path of moisture  ingress into the PA die.

Nano-fluorescent dye penetration test was used to  determine the possible moisture ingress point that resulted in  the delamination, corrosion and metal migration in the PA die . The experiment included below steps, which are illustrated in  Fig. 4.

1) Sample pre-treatment: Wipe the chip with organic  solvent to make its surface clean, and observe with an optical  microscope to ensure that the sample is clean and free of dirt.

2) Synthesis and preparation of fluorescent ion solution:  Select   specific   fluorescent   ions   according   to   the  characteristics of the sample, prepare a fluorescent ion  solution with a certain concentration, and store it in a dark  place.

3) Sample processing: Immerse the sample in the  fluorescent ion solution for a certain period, so that the  fluorescent ions can fully enter the ultra-fine cracks or  pinhole defects in the sample; after the sample is taken out,  rinse it with the corresponding solvent and then dry it.

4) Ultra-fine crack or pinhole defect location: According  to the characteristics of the sample, a super-resolution  fluorescence microscope is used to track the fluorescent ions  entering the sample defect, and the fluorescence microscopic  picture is obtained to locate the defect. The optical inspection  should be in a dark laboratory [1].

## I.    CONCLUSIONS 

In this study, Nano-Fluorescent dye penetration test was  performed to determine the possible moisture ingress point  that resulted in the delamination and metal migration in the

Power Amplifier die. Sign of fluorescent residue was  observed on the PA die surface after soaking in fluorescent  dye. Further FIB cross sections were performed and  confirmed there are cracks on the Si 3 N 4  layer and  delamination on the BCB layer. The Si3N4 crack and BCB  delamination highlighted with presence of fluorescent  residues allowed clear interpretation of moisture ingression  path into the die and caused the delamination, corrosion and  metal migration that resulted in the Icc leakage failure. This  work shows that Nano-Fluorescent dye method is a useful and  effective failure analysis technique to detect surface defect of  the passivation layer of a die.
