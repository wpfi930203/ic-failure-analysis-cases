## A novel SCF H 2 O process increasing HfO 2  MOSCAP reliability  

Chuan-Wei Kuo 1 ,   Tsung-Ming Tsai 1 , Ting-Chang Chang 2 , Hong-Yi Tu 1 , Sheng-Yao Chou 1

1 Department of Materials and Optoelectronic Science, National Sun Yat-Sen University, Kaohsiung, 80424, Taiwan.  2 Department of Physics, National Sun Yat-Sen University, Kaohsiung, 80424, Taiwan.

Phone : +886 916-551-172, Email : bike615353@gmail.com

A bstract — The study investigated the reliability of ALDHfO2 dielectric MOSCAP after H 2  annealing, SCF H 2 , and  SCF H 2 O treatments. Experimental results showed that,  after SCF  $\pmb{H z o}$   treatment,   $\pmb{D i t}$   decreased and reliability under  RTA process at   $\pmb{400^{\circ}C}$   improved. This phenomenon may be  attributed to the stronger dissociation of Si-OH bonds  compared to Si-H bonds. The NBTI experiment further  confirmed the model. In addition, comparing the CET  changes after the three process conditions, it can be  confirmed that there is no additional CET contribution due  to water after SCF H 2 O treatment. Finally, XPS analysis was  used to confirm that the proportion of Si-OH bonds of  device with the SCF   $\pmb{H z o}$   treatment increased at the  $\pmb{S i V S i O_{2}}$    interface. Based on the experimental results, it is  suggested that SCF H 2 O is advantageous for applications  in advanced process MOSFET defect passivation, reducing  interface defects, enhancing reliability, and prolonging chip  lifetime.

Keywords—Metal Oxide Semiconductor Capacitors  (MOSCAP), Supercritical fluid (SCF), bond energy, Negative  Bias Temperature Instability (NBTI)

## I.   I NTRODUCTION 

S  ince the 1960s, semiconductor technology has undergone  rapid development over the span of 60 years. In 1975,  Gordon Moore, the proposer of Moore's Law [1], predicted  that the number or efficiency of transistors on integrated circuits  would double every two years. Currently, over   $99\%$   of  integrated circuits utilize metal-oxide-semiconductor fieldeffect transistors (MOSFETs), which offer advantages such as  low cost, low power consumption, and high compatibility with  integrated circuits (ICs). MOSFET technology has become a  hallmark of technological evolution.

In advanced manufacturing  processes, reducing device leakage is a crucial issue [5].  Leakage can lead to additional heat generation, thereby  diminishing the overall reliability of the chip. The drain leakage  is affected by factors such as gate control capability, and  semiconductor interface defects   $\left(\mathrm{{Dit}\right)}$  . In traditional processes  [6],  $\mathrm{H}_{2}$   annealing is utilized to generate Si-H bonds at interfaces  after the deposition of insulator layer, reducing D it . However,  Si-H bonds have lower strength which cause the bond break in  the process due to the heat, leading to an increase in   $\mathrm{Dit}$  . In  advanced processes, the manufacturing process becomes more  complex. A higher thermal budget during the process is  generated, resulting in severe Si-H bond breakage. Supercritical fluid  (SCF) refers to the state of a substance at a temperature and  pressure above its critical point, exhibiting unique material  properties between the liquid and gas states. The high solubility  and permeability of SCF make it effective for dissolving solutes  and diffusing into devices. In SCF-related research,   $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$   is the  most commonly used SCF treatment solvent due to its low  critical temperature, inert characteristics, and environmental  friendliness. ## III.   R ESULTS AND  D ISCUSSION   

First, compare the oxide leakage currents under   $\mathrm{H}_{2}$   annealing,  SCF H 2 , and SCF H 2 O process conditions, as shown in Fig. 3(b).  When the negative bias is applied on p-type MOSCAP, the  holes accumulate on the surface and the current is measured as  the oxide leakage current. The leakage current at 2 MV/cm was   $2.5{\times}10^{-6}\,\mathrm{A}/\mathrm{cm}^{2}$  . In comparison with previous literature [4], the  ultra-thin   $\mathrm{{HfO}_{2}}$   oxide layer in this study exhibits excellent  characteristics. Furthermore, after SCF   $\mathrm{H}_{2}$   and SCF   $\mathrm{H}_{2}\mathrm{O}$  treatments, a reduction in leakage current can be observed  comparing to the   $\mathrm{H}_{2}$  annealing. In previous literature, oxygen  vacancies may be produced in  $\mathrm{{HfO}_{2}}$   and   $\mathrm{SiO}_{2}$   thin films during  the deposition[10]. Oxygen are beneficial for repairing oxygen  vacancies in   SiO 2 and both hydrogen and oxygen are beneficial  for repairing oxygen vacancies in   $\mathrm{HfO_{2}[11\mathbf{-}13]}$  , therefore, the  device with SCF   $\mathrm{H}_{2}$   treatment has more hydrogen available to  repair the oxygen vacancies. Additionally, the number of  oxygen vacancies is reduced under the effects of hydrogen and  oxygen for the device with SCF  $\mathrm{H}_{2}\mathrm{O}$   treatment, resulting in a  decrease in the leakage current of the oxide layer of the  MOSCAP.

Next, the impact of SCF treatment on the interface density state   $\left({{\bf{D}}_{\mathrm{{it}}}}\right)$   is investigated. According to the physics of semiconductor  devices, subthreshold swing (SS) is determined by interface  quality, as expressed as follows [1]:

where k is the Boltzmann coefficient,   $\mathbf{q}$   is the electron charge,   $\mathrm{C_{ox}}$   is the gate oxide capacitance,   $\mathrm{C_{D}}$   is the depletion  capacitance, and   $\mathbf{\mathrm{C_{\mathrm{it}}=\mathbf{q}\cdot D_{\mathrm{it}}}}$   is the capacitance related to the  response of the interface traps at the channel interface [14-15].

When the  $\mathrm{Dit}$   at  $\mathrm{Si}/\mathrm{SiO}_{2}$   interface is low, the SS is small. A lower  SS results in reduced drain leakage current, thereby lowering  power consumption. The conductance technique was used to  determine the  $\mathrm{Dit}$  . Capacitance-voltage (C-V) and conductancevoltage (G-V) are electrical characteristics that common utilize  to extract important parameters to understand of MOS capacitor  in research and development. Such as the fixed charge (Q f ),  CET and dielectric/channel  $\mathrm{D_{it}}$  .

In 1967, the conductance method was proposed by Nicollian  Goetzberger, is one of the most sensitive methods to determine  D it  [16-18]. D it  of   $10^{9}\,\#/\mathrm{{cm}}^{2}\mathrm{{eV}}$   or lower can be measured. The  technique is based on measuring the equivalent parallel  conductance (G P ) of an MOS capacitor as a function of bias  voltage and frequency. Conductance represents the loss  mechanism cause by the trapping and emission of carriers by  interface traps.

When the  annealing temperature reaches   $400^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$  , Si-H bond breakage  occurs which leading an increased   $\mathrm{D_{it}}$  . Moreover, previous studies have shown  that after NBTI operation, hydrogen near the interface may rebond with Si, causing the recovered degradation. ## IV.   C ONCLUSION 

This study compares the effects of supercritical   $\mathrm{H}_{2}$   and   $\mathrm{H}_{2}\mathrm{O}$  treatments on ultra-thin ALD   $\mathrm{{HfO}_{2}}$   dielectric MOSCAP. The  electrical characteristics such as leakage and   $\mathrm{Dit}$   of MOSCAP  devices after H 2  annealing, SCF H 2 , and SCF   $\mathrm{H}_{2}\mathrm{O}$   process are  measured. Experimental results indicate that both SCF   $\mathrm{H}_{2}$   and  SCF  $\mathrm{H}_{2}\mathrm{O}$   treatments are more effective in reducing oxide layer  leakage. Additionally, devices treated with SCF   $\mathrm{H}_{2}\mathrm{O}$   exhibit

better stability after RTA process at   $400^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$   due to the Si-OH  bonds dissociation energy is higher than SiH bonds. Further  evidences from NBTI reliability experiment and XPS analysis  also suggest that SCF  $\mathrm{H}_{2}\mathrm{O}$   treatment forms Si-OH bonds at the  Si/SiO 2  interface. Based on the experiment results, the SCF   $\mathrm{H}_{2}\mathrm{O}$   treatment can improve the devices reliability and thus  conducive to maintaining stable chips.
