## Conclusions and perspectives 

▪  Reliable memristive switching behaviors have been observed in low-dimensional  based nanomemristors, exhibiting  threshold RS with low variability , synaptic  plasticity, etc.

▪  C-AFM generated controllable direct contact is a reliable technique to detect  multiple electrical signals  at the nanoscale , which provides an effective approach  for  measuring scaling electronic devices  in the More Than Moore.

▪  Developing 2D technology can enable further scaling down with low energy  consumption, numerous fundamental challenges (e.g.,  scalable growth methods,  high-uniformity, high device yield and high reliability ) still need to be  addressed to meet the standards for industrial applications.
