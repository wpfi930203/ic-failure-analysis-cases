Abstract-   Physical realization of electrical synapses with good  reliability aroused great interest due to rapid development of  neuromorphic   computing.   Meanwhile,   a   new   family   of  two-dimensional transition metal carbides (TMC) has exhibited  great potential in performance improvement of electronic devices.  However, there are very few investigations for performing  synaptic devices using transition metal carbides. Moreover,  reliability is crucial for achieving good biological plasticity as well  as a controllable synaptic system. In this work, synaptic devices  with transition metal carbide were successfully fabricated and  reliability has been studied in details. Resistance window of  memristor has been able to achieve 7 orders, which provides a  possible solution in building artificial neural networks with  accurate and efficient learning capability.

## I. I NTRODUCTION 

In this work, we fabricated memristive devices with  transition metal carbide followed by electrical measurement,  physical characterization, reliability and biological plasticity  testing. Synapse-like long term plasticity and reliability were  carefully investigated. This work not only expands the  applications of transition metal carbide to the field of  memristor, but also paves the way for attaining controllable  neuromorphic devices.

## II.   EXPERIMENTS  

Fig. 1a shows the first principle calculated atomic structure of  transition metal carbide. Four scanning electron microscope  (SEM) images (in Fig. 1b, c, d) show that the shape of TMC is  like a thin piece of stacked layers. The TMC-based memristive  device fabricated on Si wafer is shown in Fig. 2a, and the size of  devices shown in Fig. 2b. The disc device stack consisted of 90  nm tungsten bottom electrode and  $80\;\mathrm{nm}\;\mathrm{SiO}_{2}$  -based switching  layers which were deposited by sputtering, followed by spin  coating of solution of TMC and water (1:150) onto   $\mathrm{SiO}_{2}$  , and  the thickness of the TMC layer is 2nm. Finally,   $100\:\mathrm{nm\,Cu}$  was  sputtered as top electrodes.

## IV. C ONCLUSION 

Memristors with transition metal carbide were used to  emulate synaptic functions. Both long-term synaptic plasticity  and reliability were investigated together with obvious analog  behaviors and a large resistance window of 7 orders.  Memristive devices proposed in this work provides a great  potential in neuromorphic applications.
