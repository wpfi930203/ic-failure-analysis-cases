## Gate-Voltage-Stress-Induced Instability in C 8 -BTBT  Thin-Film Transistors with Aluminium Oxide  

Yan Yan, Zizhen Huang, Xiaotong Ma, Zhendong Jiang,  \* Meng Zhang  Institute of Microscale Optoelectronics (IMO), Shenzhen University, Shenzhen 518060, China  \* Phone: +86-755-26925737   Fax: +86-755-26534831   Email:  zhangmeng@szu.edu.cn

Abstract-   Degradation behaviors of  $\mathbf{C_{8}}$  -BTBT thin-film transistors  (TFTs) with aluminum oxide film as gate dielectric under various  gate voltage stress are studied. A higher positive gate voltage  stress brings   a corelated higher positive shift in transfer  characteristics in  $\mathbf{C_{8}}$  -BTBT TFTs while more negative gate voltage  stress brings more negative shift. The   trapped charges and the  fixed charge generation may be responsible for the positive shift  and the negative shift respectively.

Keywords  –  Postive gate bias stress; Negative gate bias stress;    C 8 -BTBT, Transistors .

## I. I NTRODUCTION 

Organic thin film transistors (OTFTs) have attracted wide  attention and research due to their extensive applications [1-3],  such as radio frequency identification tags, biological and  optical   detection   and   flat   panel   display.   The  2,7-dioctyl[1]benzothieno[3,2-b][1]benzothiophene   (i.e.,   $\mathrm{C_{8}}$  -BTBT) is a kind of p-type molecule. Given a higher carrier  mobility   $(>40\;\mathrm{~cm}^{2}\;\mathrm{~V}^{-1}\mathrm{s}^{-1}$   for single crystal) [4, 5], good  environmental stability and optical response [6],   $\mathrm{C_{8}}$  -BTBT has  been widely studied as organic semiconductor in the OTFTs. In  addition, the  $\mathrm{C_{8}}$  -BTBT semiconductor exhibits good solubility,  and can be used in room-temperature drop-casting, spin-coating  or in-jet printing [4, 7]. Compared to traditional vacuum  deposition process, solution method is very suitable for low  cost, low temperature and large-scale fabrication. In practical  applications, the operation stability of devices is crucial, which  determines their service life time Therefore, reliability issues  must be addressed prior to its commercialization.

In this work,   $\mathrm{C_{8}}$  -BTBT TFTs with aluminium oxide   $(\mathrm{Al}_{2}\mathrm{O}_{3})$   as gate dielectric is fabricated and the reliability of   $\mathrm{C_{8}}$  -BTBT TFTs under different gate voltage stress is studied. A  decent   $\mathrm{C_{8}}$  -BTFT TFT with on/off ratio of   $6~\times~10^{5}$   and a  subthreshold swing ( SS ) of 1.06 V/dec is achieved. For the  gate-voltage-stress-induced instability, a persistent positive  shift in transfer characteristic is found for positive   $V_{g}$   while a  negative shift for negative   $V_{g}$  . The charge trapping and fixed  charge generation may be respectively responsible.

## IV. C ONCLUSION 

The   $\mathrm{C_{8}}$  -BTBT TFTs with different gate dielectrics are  fabricated and characterized. Gate-voltage-stress-induced  instability in   $\mathrm{C_{8}}$  -BTBT TFTs with   ${\mathrm{Al}}_{2}{\mathrm{O}}_{3}$   as gate dielectric is  studied. More positive stress   $V_{g}$   brings more positive shift in  transfer characteristics while more negative stress   $V_{g}$   brings  more negative shift. The charge trapping and the fixed charge  generation may be responsible for the positive shift and  negative shift respectively.
