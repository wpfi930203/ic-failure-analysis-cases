## Electrical Degradations of p-GaN HEMT under High  Off-state Bias Stress with Negative Gate Voltage  

Chi Zhang, Sheng Li, Siyang Liu, Jiaxing Wei, Wangran Wu, Weifeng Sun\*  National ASIC System Engineering Research Center, Southeast University, Nanjing, China.  \* Phone: +86 25 83795811   Fax: +86 25 83795077   Email: swffrog@seu.edu.cn

Abstract-   The shifts of electrical parameters for p-GaN high  electron mobility transistor (HEMT) under high off-state bias  stress with negative gate voltage are investigated in this paper.  Comparing to the conventional off-state bias stress with zero gate  voltage, a more significant positive shift of threshold voltage and a  more evident decrease of gate leakage current were observed  under negative gate bias stress condition. The dominant  degradation mechanism is suggested that electron traps induced  by high electric field at gate-source access region capture more  electrons in the barrier layer with the increasing negative gate bias.  This work is of significance for the researches on the long-term  reliability of the practical system using p-GaN HEMT device.

Keywords  –  p-GaN Gate, HEMT, off-state stress, negative gate  bias, electrical field.

## I. I NTRODUCTION 

With the increasing progress of power electronics, the power  converters such as high-speed DC/DC conversions are widely  used in automotive, military, industrial and photovoltaic  systems [1]-[3]. Comparing to the Si-based laterally diffused  metal oxide semiconductor transistors (Si-LDMOS) used in  DC/DC converters, GaN-based high electron mobility  transistors (HEMTs) are promising better candidates owing to  their high switching speed and low switching losses. However,  due to the special operation characteristics and relatively small  threshold voltage   $\mathrm{(V_{th})}$   of the p-GaN HEMT devices, negativepower shutdown is often used in DC/DC conversion  applications to prevent the devices from error turning-on for  system noise [4]. In this case, some electrical parameters will  degrade under negative gate-bias stress for such a long-time  operation.

Off-state stress induced the electrical parameters instabilities  of the p-GaN HEMTs have been widely reported in many  published articles .  Several possible mechanisms have been  suggested, including the trapping behaviour chiefly caused by  negative charging in the state of surface [5]; the inverse  piezoelectric effect induced defect formation in the AlGaN  barrier at the gate edge [6]; the gate electrons are injected and  trapped in the AlGaN barrier by tunnelling-assisted PooleFrenkel conduction mechanism [7].  In system applications, the  gate voltage is often applied at negate value.  The negative gate  bias stress has been found to exert a more significant on the  electrical parameter. However, this phenomenon is less studied.

In this work, degradation mechanism especially under the  high off-state voltage with negative gate bias have been

discussed. Thereby, this work has guiding significance for the  device design and the related system stability considerations.

II.   D EVICE  S TRUCTURE AND  E XPERIMENTAL  P ROCEDURE

A 15V/3.4A commercial p-GaN HEMT (EPC2040) from  Efficient Power Conversion Corporation is studied in this work  [8]. The typical   $\mathrm{V_{th}}$   and on-state resistance   $\mathrm{(R_{on})}$   at room  temperature are   1.4V   and    $30\mathrm{m}\Omega$  , respectively.   The  representative cross-section structure of p-GaN HEMT studied  in this paper is presented in Fig. 1.

Both the stress procedures and electrical parameter  measurements are accomplished by using Agilent B1505A  semiconductor characterization tracer. All the measurement  programs are executed immediately after each stress period to  ensure the accuracy of the experimental results.

In this paper, four different negative gate-bias voltage  conditions (-4V, -2V, -1V and 0V) and four different drain-bias  voltage conditions (0V, 4V, 8V and 12V) are regarded. The  stress tests are interrupted selectively to monitor the  degradations of the electrical parameters. All the experiments  and measurements are carried out at room temperature.

To understand the above phenomena, the extracted  perpendicular electrical field (PEF) along the AlGaN/GaN  interface is given in Fig. 6. The positive peak PEF beneath the  bottom of p-GaN cap layer leads to the electrons trapping into  the barrier layer [11]-[13]. With the increase of negative gate  bias voltage, the positive peak PEF beneath the bottom of pGaN cap layer near source side    $\left(\mathrm{x=1.8upm}\right)$   increases  significantly, causing more electrons trapped into the barrier  layer, which takes the responsibility for the increase of   $\mathrm{V_{th}}$  and  the rise of   $\mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{on}}$   under low   $\mathrm{V_{gs}}$  condition. The peak value of PEF  beneath the bottom of gate region   $(\mathbf{x{=}2\upmu m})$   increases, but the  region under p-GaN cap is rarely influenced due to that the  electrons in this region are depleted by p-GaN, indicating few  electrons trapped into the barrier layer. Meanwhile, the peak  PEF of the other side   $(\mathrm{x}{=}3.4\upmu\mathrm{m})$   is almost unchanged with the

increasing negative gate voltage, which does confirm that the  negative gate voltage stress rarely affects the gate-drain assess  region. Moreover, the gate-drain assess region resistance  occupies main portion of the total resistance, weakening the  influence of trapped electrons on the  $\mathrm{R_{on}}$   when it is under high   $\mathrm{V_{gs}}$  .

## III. C ONCLUSION 

In conclusion, the electrical degradations of p-GaN HEMT  device under high off-state bias stress with negative gate  voltage have been investigated in this work. A more significant  positive shift of   $\mathrm{V_{th}}$   and a more evident decrease of   $\mathrm{I_{gss}}$   were  observed under negative gate bias stress condition. This  phenomenon can be explained by electron trapping mechanism.  As the increase of negative gate voltage, the electric field in  gate-source access region is enhanced, more electron traps are  activated. Therefore, the electrons in channel region are more  easily captured by the activated traps in barrier layers. It is the  trapped electrons that increase the   $\mathrm{V_{th}}$    and decrease the   $\mathrm{I_{gs}.}$  . This  degradation is often ignored in the practical applications.  Consequently, when the practical lifetime of the p-GaN HEMT  is evaluated, the negative gate bias stress of must be considered,  especially for the high-speed DC/DC conversion application.
