## Simultaneous 2D carrier polarity (dC/dV) and density  (dC/dz) distribution measurement of Si/SiC MOSFET  based on scanning nonlinear dielectric microscopy   

T. Yamaoka 1, T. Aiso 1, K. Watanabe 2, Y. Shikakura 2, T. Ueno 2, K. Tamura 3, K. Mizuguchi 3\*  1 Application Development Dept., Hitachi High-Technologies Corporation, Takatsu-ku, Kawasaki-shi, 213-0012, Japan.  2 Design Dept., Hitachi High-Tech Science Corporation, Oyama-cho, Sunto-gun, 410-1313, Japan.   3 Marketing Dept., Hitachi High-Technologies Corporation, NishiShimbashi, Tokyo, 227-0033, Japan.  \*Phone: +81-3-6758-2136 \*Email:  takehiro.yamaoka.jv@hitachi-hightech.com

Abstract-    As   a   semiconductor   2D   carrier   distribution  measurement   method,   we   have   developed   simultaneous  measurement technology of dC/dV (polarity) and dC/dz (density)  based on SNDM. This method has been applied to IMEC's dopant  step sample and Si power MOSFET sample to confirm the  monovalent functional property of the dC/dz and the signal profile  according to the carrier density. Furthermore, this method has  been also applied to the SiC planar MOSFET. As a result, the  dC/dz monovalent functional property in a wide carrier density  range of   $\mathbf{p}^{\mathrm{~++}}$   (Poly-Si gate), n + (Source), p (Body), n- (Epi) has  been shown.

Keywords  –   SNDM, carrier distribution, carrier density (carrier  concentration) .

## I. I NTRODUCTION 

Both SCM (Scanning Capacitance Microscopy) and SNDM  (Scanning Nonlinear Dielectric Microscopy) [1] are available  to determine either p-type or n-type polarity of the  semiconductor, and those techniques measure dC/dV of the  capacitance variations of the depletion layer while applying the  AC voltage between the probe and the sample. However, an  artifact of “contrast reversal” has been reported as the dC/dV

output and it becomes bivalent function as carrier-density [2].  Recently, Hirose  et al.  proposed the SNDM-dC/dz which is  capacitance change induced by cantilever vibration as a  solution for contrast reversal [3].

We have been studying environmental control SNDM  (Model: HS-SNDM /AFM5300E , Hitachi High-Tech) and  have succeeded in low density observation of   $10^{15-14}\,\mathrm{cm}^{-3}$   level.  Furthermore, environmental dependence of SNDM response in  air / dry N 2  / high vacuum has been investigated [4].  Furthermore, we have developed two methods that can  simultaneously measure the topography, contact dC/dV and  non-contact dC/dz    at the same time. One is "SNDM - Trace  mode" which switches the measurement for each scanning line.  The other is "SNDM - SIS (Sampling Intelligent Scan [5])  mode" which switches for each pixel [6].

In this issue, we show the results of applying to Si/SiC  MOSFET   cross   section   samples   using   simultaneous  measurements of dC/dV and dC/dz.

## II.   EXPERIMENTS  

Fig.   1(a) is the block diagram of instrumentation for  obtaining the dC/dV and the dC/dz signals. Fig.   1 (b) is the  moving trajectory of the probe in the SNDM-Trace mode that  switches contact- dC/dV and noncontact-dC/dz measurement  line by line. Fig. 1 (c) is the moving trajectory of the probe in  the SNDM-SIS mode that switches contact-dC/dV and  noncontact-dC/dz measurement for each pixel. In the case of  the SNDM-SIS mode, the acquisition point of the dC/dz signal  is determined as the local maximum value of dC/dz in the  region where the probe approached the sample.

## IV. C ONCLUSION 

As a semiconductor 2D carrier distribution measurement  method, we have developed simultaneous measurement  technology of dC/dV (polarity) and dC/dz (density) based on  SNDM. And we have applied it to IMEC's dopant step sample  and Si power MOSFET sample in order to confirm the  monovalent functional property of the   $\mathrm{{d}C/\mathrm{{d}z}}$   and the signal  profile depending on the carrier density. It is suggested that the  dC/dz measurement may develop into quantitative carrier  density analysis using dopant standard samples. In addition, we  have applied it to the SiC planar MOSFET. As a result, the   $\mathrm{{d}C/\mathrm{{d}z}}$   monovalent functional property in a wide carrier density  range of p ++ (Poly-Si gate), n + (Source), p (Body), n- (Epi)  has been shown.

In this issue, the experimental results are described using the  mechanically polished samples. Ohta  et al . have reported actual  examples of applying SNDM to failure analysis by utilizing

Focused Ion Beam (FIB) sample preparation technique [8]. It is  a very effective way of the location/device specific requirement  for failure analysis. Furthermore, Hirota  et al . have achieved  SNDM resolution of 10 nm or less with a super sharp diamond  probe [7].

We believe a combination of these sample preparation  techniques, high-resolution probes, and newly developpd the  SNDM-dC/dV and dC/dz measurement technologies will be  extremely useful tools for next-generation failure analysis.
