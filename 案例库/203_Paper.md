## Investigation of Electrical Parameters Degradations  for 600V SOI-LIGBT under Repetitive ESD Stresses  

Li Lu, Ran Ye, Siyang Liu, Weifeng Sun \*   National ASIC System Engineering Research Center, Southeast University, Nanjing, China.  \* Phone: +86 25 8795811   Fax: +86 25 83795077 Email: swffrog@seu.edu.cn

Abstract-    Electrical   parameters   degradations   of  silicon-on-insulator (SOI) lateral insulated-gate bipolar transistor  (LIGBT) under repetitive electrostatic discharge (ESD) stresses  have been investigated. After the repetitive ESD stresses, the  degradation of threshold voltage   $(\mathbf{V_{th}})$   can be neglected due to the  intact channel region. The decrease of on-resistance   $(\mathbf{R_{on}})$   is  dominated by hot holes injection into the field oxide at the bird’s  beak. Moreover, the saturation current (I ce,sat ) is decreased  dramatically because of hot holes injection and interface states  generation at the poly-gate edge. Finally, a novel structure with an  additional P-type region beneath the poly-gate edge has been  proposed to suppress the device degradation under repetitive ESD  stresses.

Keywords  – Repetitive   Electrostatic discharge (ESD) stresses,  Electrical parameter degradation, Lateral insulated-gate bipolar  trasistor (LIGBT)

## I. I NTRODUCTION 

Silicon-on-insulator (SOI) lateral insulated-gate bipolar  transistor (LIGBT) is widely used as output device in  monolithic smart power ICs due to its high breakdown voltage,  high current density and easy integration, such as DC brush  motor, switching power supplies and automotive applications  [1] - [4]. The output terminal without electrostatic discharge  (ESD) protection circuits is usually carried out because of  limited chip area. In this way, the SOI-LIGBT will easily suffer  random ESD from external circumstance. Therefore, the study  of ESD reliability for the LIGBT is significant. Recent  researches more focus on the devices’ ESD robustness and their  optimal design [5-7]. Actually, the device may not immediately  fail under the single or repetitive weaker ESD stresses, but its  electrical characteristic has degraded already, while barely  reported. Therefore, the electrical parameters degradations of  SOI-LIGBT have been studied under repetitive ESD stresses in  this paper.

## II.   S TRUCTURES AND EXPERIMENTS 

Fig. 1 shows the schematic cross section of the investigated  device. It is fabricated with a   $0.5{\mathrm{-}}{\upmu\mathrm{m}}$   CMOS high-voltage  process on silicon-on-insulator substrate. The main structure  parameters are indicated as follows: the thickness of gate oxide  is 50nm. The effective length of channel region and drift region  are  $2.6\upmu\mathrm{m}$   and  $66\upmu\mathrm{m}$  , respectively. The threshold voltage   $\mathrm{(V_{th})}$  and off-state breakdown voltage   $(\mathrm{BV}_{\mathrm{off}})$   are 2.6V and 697V,  respectively. The operating gate-emitter voltage   $(\mathrm{V_{ge}})$   and  collector-emitter voltage   $(\mathrm{V_{ce}})$   are 15V and 600V, respectively.

Transmission line pulse (TLP) with 10ns rising / falling time

and 100ns holding time is an effective method to evaluate ESD  robustness [8-10] and its experimental results are presented in  Fig. 2. It is can be seen that the secondary breakdown current   $\left(\mathrm{I}_{2}\right)$   of the investigated SOI-LIGBT is 0.5A. Then, the 90  percent of I t2  (0.45A) has been selected as standard TLP stress  which is applied on the device’s collector with grounding other  electrodes. The TLP stress is carried out one by one to form a  repetitive TLP stresses. During the experiment, the electrical  parameters including   $\mathrm{V_{th}},$   on-resistance   $\mathrm{(R_{on})}$  ,   and saturation  current   $(\mathrm{I_{cc,sat}})$   are monitored immediately after the repetitive  TLP stresses.

III. M EASUREMENTS AND DISCUSSIONS

Fig. 3 shows the transfer and output characteristic curves  before and after the repetitive TLP stresses. It is can be found  that the  $\mathrm{{I_{ce,sat}}}$   owns an obvious degradation as increasing times

of TLP stress but the   $\mathrm{V_{th}}$   is not. Furthermore, the   $\mathrm{R_{on}}$    degradation cannot be observed intuitively from the transfer  curves. Therefore, the variation tendencies of  $\mathrm{V_{th}}$  ,   $\mathrm{R_{on}}$   and  $\mathrm{{I_{ce,sat}}}$  versus the times of TLP stress have been extracted further in  Fig.4. The degradation of  $\mathrm{V_{th}}$   is basically below   $1\%$   that can be  ignored. However, the degradations of   $\mathrm{R_{on}}$   and   $\mathrm{{I}_{c e,s a t}}$  decrease  monotonously as the increasing times of TLP stress and the   $\mathrm{{I_{ce,sat}}}$   decreases more seriously than   $\mathrm{R_{on}}$  . To reveal the inner  mechanisms of above-mentioned phenomena, the technology  computer aided design (TCAD) has been employed in this  work.

Fig. 5 shows the distribution of 2D impact ionization (I.I.)  rate under the 0.45A TLP stress. In the device surface, three  evident I.I. rate peaks can be observed and they are located at  the bird’s beak   $(\mathrm{X=}10.5\upmu\mathrm{m})$  , poly-gate edge   $\left(\mathrm{X=}26\upmu\mathrm{m}\right)$   and  emitter-metal edge   $\scriptstyle(X=51\,\upmu\mathrm{m})$  ), respectively. Moreover, no I.I.  rate peaks can be found in the channel region, which means no  damage there. That is why the   $\mathrm{V_{th}}$   changes few after repetitive  TLP stresses.

Fig. 6 shows the distributions of I.I. rate, perpendicular  electric field (PEF) and hole temperature along the   $\mathrm{{SiO}_{2}/\mathrm{{Si}}}$    interface under the 0.45A TLP stress. Obviously, the I.I. rate

peak also occurs at those three regions (the bird’s beak,  poly-gate edge and emitter-metal edge). Meanwhile, high hole  temperature and strong positive (the direction is pointing to  device surface) perpendicular electric field are found there.  These crucial factors are in favor of hot holes injection and  interface states generation [11-13]. The influence of hot holes  injection and interface states generation in those three regions  on   $\mathrm{R_{on}}$   and   $\mathrm{{I_{ce,sat}}}$   will be analyzed in next work.

Firstly, the distributions of hole and electron current density  along Y axis have been extracted at the three damage points  under the   $\mathrm{R_{on}}$   monitoring condition   $(\mathrm{V_{cc}{=}1V\&V_{g e}{=}15V})$  , as  indicated in Fig. 7(a). It is can be seen that the total current is  converged on the device surface. Moreover, the dominant  current of total current along the device surface is electron  current which is concentrated in the bird’s beak. In this region,  the flowing electron current is increased because of the negative  mirroring charges by injecting hot holes [14]. Therefore, the  $\mathrm{R_{on}}$  is decreased after repetitive TLP stresses.

Thereby, a device with an  additional P-type region has been proposed to suppress the  damage point at the poly-gate edge, as shown in Fig. 8. The  additional P-type region is formed with the same mask of  P-body. The width (W) of additional P-type region is   $0.5\upmu\mathrm{m}$  according to the design rule of technology. ## IV. O PTIMIZATION AND VERIFICATION 

Obviously, the decreasing  $\mathrm{{I_{ce,sat}}}$   is harmful to the investigated  device while the decreasing   $\mathrm{R_{on}}$  is beneficial. So the real  approach to suppress device degradation is to alleviate   $\mathrm{{I_{ce,sat}}}$

## V. C ONCLUSION 

In this paper, the electrical parameters degradations have  been investigated for SOI-LIGBT under repetitive ESD stresses.  The decrease of   $\mathrm{R_{on}}$    is attributed to hot holes injection at the

bird’s beak, while the   $\mathrm{V_{th}}$    degradation is negligible due to an  intact channel. The   $\mathrm{{I_{ce,sat}}}$    decrease is mainly caused by the hot  holes injection and interface states at the poly-gate edge.  Because the   $\mathrm{{I_{ce,sat}}}$   degradation is harmful for the investigated  device under repetitive ESD stresses, therefore, the structure  with an additional P-type region at the poly-gate edge region  has been proposed to alleviate   $\mathrm{{I_{ce,sat}}}$  degradation. Meanwhile,  other electrical parameters are almost unchanged for the  proposed device.
