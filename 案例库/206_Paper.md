## EMMI Abnormal Hotspot Study for Latch up  Simulation  

Huang Chia-Sheng, Guo Xuan-Chao, Chen Zhi-Wei, Lin shin-chia, Zhang Sheng-Ru, Tsai Bo-an  Powerchip Semiconductor Manufacturing Corporation,   No. 18 Li-Hsin Rd. 1, Hsin Chu Science Park, Hsin Chu, Taiwan, R.O.C.   \* Phone: +65-3-57121-57  EXT 4981 Fax: +65-3-57121-79   Email:  vitohuang@powerchip.com ,  rod@powerchip.com ,  monster@powerchip.com , ,  ottolin@powerchip.com ,  stacyc@powerchip.com ,  batsaia@psc.com.tw

Abstract - Latch-Up is a common circuit design problem in product  reliability. Because of the poor design of the integrated circuit, the  P-N-P-N junction in the chip is triggered by an applied current or  voltage then to happen an internal latching current. The high  temperature generated by the internal latching current will burn  the internal circuit or cause the circuit to work abnormally. This  paper is to discuss when the IC happen latch-up, we need to  prepare different test sample ex. “de-cap package sample front  side sample” / “de-cap package sample back side COB (chip on  board) sample”/ Blue tape COB backside sample. We also  compare the difference between each test samples. Finally we  discuss EMMI (Emission Microscope) hot spot experiment for  latch-up simulation that needs limited of current with each  different COB sample. The stress current limitation will induce  EMMI result, based on this fail model and improve the product  Latch-up performance by reducing parasitic BJT (bipolar  transistor) circuit current gain factor ß.

Keywords  –  Latch-up, Emission Microscope .

## I. I NTRODUCTION

The latch-up effect  is not obvious in the process of large line width, and the smaller  line width, the lower the reaction voltage of the parasitic  transistor, and occur  more obvious the latch-up effect.  Latch-Up is to verify the failure of semiconductor devices  caused by voltage transients and current transients. The test  project mainly includes two aspects, power supply voltage  overvoltage test (device breakdown voltage or 1.5 times the  maximum operating voltage) and positive and negative current  test of input and output pins Fig.1 [2].

## II. Methodology of Experiment  

When latch-up occurs, firstly need to find out the conditions  of the latch. Such as failure mechanism, pad location, stress  voltage/current etc. Finding the abnormal hot spot position on  the EMMI machine and according to the abnormal hot spot  position we can analyze IC circuit precisely. But it often affects  the whole experiment result because of the different methods of  sample preparation.

The following are three different sample conditions and  analysis results for the product fail experiment discussion.

1. Package de-cap sample :

Package de-cap sample method is generally the most  common. Generally, de-cap have separate half-opening and  full-opening methods. This article uses a half-opening method.  In order to keep the good connection between the package and  the lead-frame, you need to confirm the Wire-bond material ex.  gold wire / copper wire / silver wire then de-cap can be used  with the appropriate acid to completely retain the original gold  wire on the De-cap chip. The completed de-cap of full-opening  sample as [fig2-left]. This sample can be used in the EMMI  machine for related latch-up simulation to find out the hotspot  highlights. Half/Full-opening de-cap samples can only be  analyzed in Front-side in EMMI, so after finding hotspot, often  because the latch-up occurs in the peripheral circuit and pad  area, the detected hot spot will be indirectly by metal. The layer  is blocked and the exact circuit position cannot be found  [fig2-right]. Therefore, EMMI analysis is required from the  sample back-side to find the correct position, and after the  EMMI analysis, the related package electrical test is performed  to confirm that the electrical properties are the same as those of  the unopened package.

## IV. Conclusion  

Latch-up refers to the low impedance path generated between  the power VDD and the ground GND (VSS) due to the  interaction between the parasitic PNP and the NPN bipolar BJT  in the CMOS chip. When the IC is working normally, it is not

PMOS open NMOS off or NMOS open PMOS off, but the  NMOS,PMOS is on, then the inverter will not work  properly.( There is no reverse effect) Output OUT that point  PMOS NMOS current is in Fighting. At this time, the current  flows from Vcc to VSS. The problem of current limiting caused  an abnormality in the position of the Pad A / Pad B hotspot in  this experiment. We explained the cause of the abnormality  through the circuit profile. :

Act1. Pad A +stress induce parasitic BJT P-N-P turn-on  Act2. (Current limitation<150mA) LU happen on Pad A-GND

Therefore, we must prevent the occurrence of latch-up,  reduce the BJT gain, and make latch-up not easy to occur.  Generally, there are several methods in the circuit design to  prevent latch up.

1. Guard ring:  $\mathrm{P}+$   ring surrounds PMOS and connects to GND;   $\Nu+$   ring surrounds PMOS and connects to VDD, which can  reduce the resistance of R-well and R-sub, and prevent carriers  from reaching the base of BJT. If possible, add two more rings.

[4]  Jung-Ruey Tsai, Yi-Sheng Chang, Jui-Chang Lin, Shu-Ming Bai, Gene  Sheu , Shao-Ming Yang, Chun-Hsien Wu, Hsueh-Chun Liao, Ruey-Dar Chang,  “Improvement of CMOS latch-up in bootstrapping circuit application” 2014  International Symposium on Next-Generation Electronics (ISNE).

2. Deepen Isolation. Is to add isolation between NMOS and  PMOS

3. Design rule increases  $\Nu+$  ,  $\mathrm{P}+$   distance. After the design time,  the distance of   $\mathrm{P}+$  ,  $\Nu+$  , the distance of the guard ring from   $\mathrm{P}+$  ,   $\Nu+$  , etc. will be specified.

4. SOI. Silicon on Insulator, adding a layer of SiO2 on the  surface of Si, so that well or   $\Nu+$   can’t be directly connected to  P-sub, so that electrons or holes can’t get below.

5. High-energy ion implantation is used to drive impurities into  the bottom of the well. This trap is not the same as the  conventional well surface, and the bottom of the well has the  lowest concentration, but the opposite is true, so it is called an  inverted well. This concept is extremely important! The  concentration below is very large then after the electron or hole  reaches the base, the high-concentration deep well can  effectively increase the recombination, so you don't want to go  to the collector, lower the bipolar amplification factor, so that  the transistor without back bias is free from latch- Up. All of the  above methods are trying to reduce the ß so that the parasitic  BJT is not easy to turn-on.
