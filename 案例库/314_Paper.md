## Impact of low temperature on the TSG Vt shift during  erase cycling of 3-D NAND Flash memory  

Da Li 1,3 , Lei Jin 2,3 , Liang Yan 2,3 , Xinlei Jia 2,3 , Jianquan Jia 3 , Yali Song 3 An Zhang 3 , Feng  $\mathrm{Xu}^{3}$  , Wei Hou 2,3 , Zongliang Huo 2,3 , Jianhua Feng \*1   1 Peking University, Beijing 100871, China  2 University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing 100049, China  3 Yangtze Memory Technologies Co., Ltd., Wuhan 430205, China  \*Phone: 13161661826 Email: fengjh@pku.cn

Abstract-   Charge trapping memory (CTM) endurance has been  widely investigated in recent years. Most studies are focused on  array cell Vt instabilities, which is originated from charge  trapping/detrapping in cell tunnel oxide and interface traps. Our  previous works demonstrate erase only cycling induced TSG shift  in 3D NAND flash. In this work, it is found that the erase cycling  induced TSG VT shift is temperature dependent.  TSG Vt shift  under low temperature is obviously worse than room and high  temperature. TCAD simulation shows hot carrier induced by  channel potential gradient is more significant under low  temperature during erase operation due to low mobility. The  stability of TSG cell Vt is related with both temperature and TSG  bias voltage during erase according to the experiments and  simulation.

Keywords  – 3D NAND Flash Memory, vertical channel, erase  scheme, hot carrier injection, cycling.

## I. INTRODUCTION  

Big data are driving memory and storage to high  performance,   high   reliability   and   optimizing   cost.  Three-dimensional (3D) NAND flash is still the major  candidate in storage technologies [1]-[4].  The reliability and  lifetime of 3D NAND flash have been intensively studied so far  [5]. Charge trapping memory (CTM) has been widely produced  because of its stable endurance and good scaling down  performance. However, to cover the increasing reliability  requirement, there are still array level reliability challenges,  such as cycling induced top select gate transistor (TSG) Vt  shift, which is reported by our previous work [6]. TSG Vt in 3D  NAND array has been reported as an important aspect for the  suppression of program disturbance of unselect string [7]. In  this work, the temperature characteristic on TSG Vt shift  during erase only cycling is studied.

In this work, the TSG Vt shift phenomenon at different  temperature was studied. It shows TSG Vt shift is more severe  in lower temperature after erase only cycling. It is because  charge trapping memory requires holes FN tunneling to achieve  erase operation and holes mobility is decreased at lower  temperature in poly-Si channel [8]. The mobility of Holes from  P-well (PW) is low when the NAND array is operated in low  temperature environment, which results in a more severe  electric field stress between bit line (BL) and TSG due to the  lower speed of holes transformation. A TCAD simulation has  been conducted well to explain the proposed mechanism.

## III.   RESULTS AND ANALYSIS  

To demonstrate this phenomenon of TSG Vt shift high at low  temperature, erase only cycling was performed for 6K times.

TSG Vt distributions are collected before and after cycling.  Meanwhile, multi blocks are tested for each case and split.  Before erase cycling, the cells in block are preprogramed to the  same level (medium  $\mathrm{V}\up t={\sim}4\mathrm{V}$  ) and the chip is preconditioned  for about 10mins in corresponding environment to make the  temperature more stable. During erase cycling, the word lines  (WLs) are biased to 0V and TSG/BSG/Dummy are biased to  predefined voltage.

It can be  observed that TSG Vt has no shift issue when TSG bias is 4V  (default) in room and high temperature. However, with TSG  bias voltage rising up, TSG Vt distributions shift rightward  gradually, no matter in which temperature. The larger TSG bias  voltage, the larger TSG Vt shifts. This is because a larger TSG  bias voltage induces higher potential drop around BL and TSG  which leads to band to band tunneling and TSG Vt shift [6]. On  the other hand, for different temperature and the same TSG bias  comparison, the gap of TSG Vt shift becomes larger when the  test was done under relatively low temperature. This indicates  that even if the default TSG bias voltage can work well at room  or high temperature, it may cause problems at low temperature.  Fig. 2(d) shows the  $+3\upsigma$   value of TSG Vt shift at different TSG  bias voltage and temperature. The trend of  $+3\upsigma$   Vt shift versus  TSG bias is the same. The Vt shift gap between pre-cycling and  post-cycling is obviously different among these three  temperatures. The lower temperature, the larger TSG Vt shifts.

## IV.   TCAD simulation  

In order to explain the phenomena, Sentaurus Sdevice  simulator is used for TCAD simulation. In the poly-silicon

channel, the Philips unified mobility model is used. It takes into  account phonon scattering, electron-hole scattering and  doping-dependent mobility degradation. Meanwhile, Lombardi  model covers the mobility degradation due to surface  roughness scattering. And thermionic emission model and  Coulomb scattering mobility model are also included in this  simulator. Fig. 3(a) shows the TCAD simulation results of  electric potential of different temperature along the poly-Si  channel when PW ramps to different voltages and TSG bias  voltage is set to 9V. Because of the 3D NAND array structure,  BL is immediately coupled up to a voltage nearly as much as  PW when it rises up [6]. On the other hand, holes from PW  takes time (longer or shorter) to transfer from the bottom to the  top WLs in the poly-Si channel, owing to the scattering  phenomena in grain boundary traps [9]. Regarding to the same  TSG bias voltage, Fig. 3(a) shows the channel potential from  PW voltage sets up more quickly under higher temperature.  Fig. 3(b)-(d) show the channel potential at  $85\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$  ,  $25\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$   and -25  $\mathrm{{}^{\circ}C}$   temperature during the erase operation. It clearly shows the  potential difference along the channel. The higher temperature,  the higher electric potential exists at the same position of the  channel during erase operation.

TCAD simulation shows the transportation of holes is  relatively slow under lower temperature, compared with room  and high temperature. BL voltage is coupled to high  immediately after PW ramps up, because of C BC . This indicates  the stress around BL and TSG (potential gradient) is more

severe under  $-25\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$  . Therefore the band to band tunneling and  hot carrier generation phenomena become worse at low  temperature.

In order to suppress the cycling induced TSG Vt shift at low  temperature, it is proposed to have temperature compensation  to the TSG bias voltage under different temperatures. When the  test chip is operating at these temperatures, TSG bias voltage  (V tsg ) is compensated to fit the temperature by millivolts per  degree   $\mathrm{(mV/^{\circ}C)}$   during erase. This scheme will compensate the  TSG bias voltage to a lower level   $(<\!\mathrm{V}\mathrm{tsg})$   under low  temperature while keeping in V tsg  when the chip is operating at  high temperature. The benefit of this proposal is using one TSG  bias voltage to cover the cycling induced TSG Vt shift in  different temperatures.

## Conclusion  

In this work, cycling induced TSG Vt shift at low  temperature has been studied. TSG Vt shift is obviously worse  under low temperature. TCAD simulation shows the  transportation of holes is relatively slow under lower  temperature, compared with room and high temperature. In  order to suppress the cycling induced TSG Vt shift higher at  low temperature, it is proposed to have temperature  compensation to the TSG bias voltage under different  temperatures.
