## The probe marker discoloration on Al pad and wafer  storage  

W.F. Hsieh, Henry Lin, Vincent Chen, Irene Ou, & Y.S. Lou  Ardentec Cooperation No. 3, Gongye 3rd Rd., Hsin-Chu Industrial Park, Hukou Township,  Hsinchu County 30351, Taiwan, R.O.C.  Phone: +886-938-699925  Fax: +886-3-5971161   Email:  michael.hsieh@ardentec.com

Abstract-   In this report, a novel probe marker discoloration  corrosion on Al pad, with donut/circle shape, was found. This  probe marker discoloration corrosion was found at the stage of  blue tape. Back to check the wafer process, no clear corrosion was  found before CP probing test. In order to identify the  microstructure & components of this probe marker discoloration  corrosion, SEM/EDS, TEM/EELS & AES analysis were applied.  These structure & surface analysis results indicated this  discoloration is a combination of Aluminum, Fluorine and  Oxygen. No Fluoride was detected on the normal region of Al pad.  All these results implied the role of fluorine element to induce this  probe marker discoloration corrosion. The source of F element is  critical in this case, so the testing and storage conditions would be  supposed to be important for the formation of probe marker  discoloration corrosion. The HAST testing with condition of  temperature  $\bf{130^{\circ}C}$   and RH  $\bf{85\%}$   for 21 hours, all the chip samples  were simulated to have been exposed in air without N2 cabinet  storage or MBB bag storage for 1 year. This HAST test result  showed none of exhibition of discoloration or existed discoloration  chips being deteriorated. Another storage test based on regular  full wafer FOSB storage showed existed discoloration chips being  deteriorated. This FOSB storage test indicated the source of  corrosive F element   maybe come from wafer itself. The EDS  element mapping data indicated the F element enriched Al pad on  wafer extreme edge area was clearly identified. The source of F  element was found. And the storage of processed wafer would play  a key role in the formation of discoloration corrosion.

Keywords  –  Al pad corrosion, probe marker discoloration,  Fluorine, wafer storage, SEM/EDS, TEM/EELS, AES, HAST.

## I. I NTRODUCTION 

The semiconductor CP (circuit probing) tests were performed  by probing on bond pad before die saw and package process.  Typical bond pad components consist of Al, Si and Cu. The  wire bond interconnection from die to external interface is the  key role in semiconductor backend package process. In the past  two decades, the quality issues afflicting bond pad in the  non-stick on pad (NSOP) failure mode were reported by some  researchers before. [1-3] One of the bond pad failure root  causes was the fluorine ( F ) contamination on Al pad surface  inducing wire interconnection reliability concern. The early  studies indicated the main F contamination root causes were  found by two ways. One is the fluorinated silicone glass (FSG),  which is a low K dielectric layer between metal layers, can  release fluorine to contaminate the Al bond pads. [1] The  second way for fluorine to react with Al metal is the fluorine  embedded from previous pad etching and cleaning gas. [2] It  was also broadly reported the wafers containing FSG layers

stored in closed containers (Front Opening Shipping Box,  FOSB) are more susceptible to F contamination by fluorine  complex.  All these studies indicated the source of fluorine  element play a critical role on Al pad corrosion. The early probe  marker discoloration study [3] reported by P. Chang had stated  the yellow discoloration Al bond pad corrosion. However, the  donut/circle shape probe marker discoloration on Al bond pad  had not been fully report yet. [4] In this paper, the novel  Fluorine-induced probe marker circle discoloration corrosion  and a compressive study will be present.

This novel probe marker discoloration was found at the stage  of blue tape.  These wafers, stayed at blue tape stage, had been  finished all the CP tests- these included CP1 and CP2 at high  temperature. And it was also found these wafers had kept more  than two month’s storage in STD FOSB after the CP tests. All  the impacted lots and wafers were then sent back to Ardentec  for root cause identification. The first inspection tool for probe  marker discoloration is the Optical Microscopy(OM). It was  found two types of probe marked discoloration corrosion  shapes formed on Al pad surface. The typical donut/circle shape  probe marker discoloration OM images with probe marker as its  discoloration area center are shown as Figure 1. And the donut  shape discoloration could be found only on few wafers. Almost  all the discoloration was shown to be circle type. However,  even in the most seriously impacted lots/wafers, it was found  the probed pads did not always induce the discoloration  corrosion. Based on   $100\%$   AVI inspection result of some  impacted wafers, it was found in the worst case, the  discoloration pads would reach up to   $15.18\%$  of all the  available dies.

II.   EXPERIMENTS   AND   RESULTS

In the investigation, chip sample with probe marked  discoloration were checked in plane-view & cross-sectional  approaches by Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM) with  Energy Dispersive Spectrum (EDS), and Transmission Electron  Microscopy (TEM) with Electron Energy Loss Spectrum  (EELS). These inspection and analysis results could get the  information of morphology and its corresponding element  components for all the normal / discoloration locations. The  Auger Electron Spectrum (AES) is a traditional surface analysis  approach for Al bond pad corrosion behavior. The surface and  its depth profiling of AES results would be reported.

To verify the impact of storage condition on the probe  marked discoloration samples, the Highly Accelerated Stress  Test (HAST) applied with test conditions of temperature at   $130\mathrm{{^oC}}$   and RH   $85\%$   for 21 hours. This test is supposed for all  the discoloration bond pad were simulated to exposure in air  without Nitrogen Cabinet storage or Moisture Barrier Bag  (MBB) storage for 1 year. The test data will be helpful for the  impacted dies corrosion behavior evaluation.  The secondary  storage test was normal/abnormal full wafer deposited in the  regular FOSB to identify the FOSB storage effect and the  source of F element.

Fig. 2 showed the top-view SEM micrograph and its  corresponding EDS analysis data of various locations. The size  of discoloration could be found to be more than 30 micro meter.  The prober marker and corresponding discoloration circle area  was easily found by SEM image. It was found in the  discoloration area, where fluorine and oxygen contents were  found on the middle of circle shape region by the EDS result.  That is to say, both the probe marker and circle area, the  discoloration is a kind of Aluminum fluorine corrosion. As for  the normal area, the Aluminum oxidation was formed.

## III. C ONCLUSION 

1.   A new probe marked discoloration with donut/circle shape  on Al bond pad was reported in this study.  2.   Based on SEM/EDS, TEM/EELS and AES analysis data,  this probe marker discoloration type Al pad corrosion was an  Aluminum fluorine-oxide forming on Al pad surface.  3.   The source of fluorine element in this corrosion case is due  to extreme edge FSG layer leakage. This is identified by EDS  mapping result from impacted wafer extreme edge area.
