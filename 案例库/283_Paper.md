## Output Breakdown Characteristics and the Related  Degradation Behaviors in Metal Oxide Thin Film  Transistors   

Xiaotong Ma 1 ,  \* Meng Zhang 1 , Zhendong Jiang 1 , Sunbin Deng 2 , Yan Yan 1 , Guijun Li 1 , Rongsheng Chen 3 ,  Man Wong 2 , Hoi-Sing Kwok 2   1 Institute of Microscale Optoelectronics (IMO), Shenzhen University, Shenzhen 518060, China  2 State Key Laboratory of Advanced Displays and Optoelectronics Technologies, The Hong Kong University of Science and  Technology, Clear Water Bay, Kowloon, Hong Kong  3 School of Electronic and Information Engineering, South China University of Technology, Guangzhou 510640, China  \* Phone: +86-755-26925737   Fax: +86-755-26534831   Email:  zhangmeng@szu.edu.cn

Abstract-   Output breakdown (OBD) characteristics of metal oxide  thin film transistors (TFTs) is studied. Three kinds of OBD  behaviors are observed, corresponding to off state, subthreshold  region and on state. The device degradation behaviors under OBD  voltage stress is investigated. OBD stress can induce severe device  degradation in very short stress time. The degradation mechanism  is tentatively discussed, incorporated with TCAD simulation.

Keywords  –  Output breakdown, Reliability, Metal oxide, Thin  film transistors .

## I. I NTRODUCTION 

Due to low cost, low-temperature process and  reasonable electrical characteristics, metal oxide (MO)  thin-film transistors (TFTs) [1] have attracted much  attention from various fields, such as flat panel displays [2],  sensors [3], and integrated circuits [4]. For practical  applications, reliability issue is always a big concern. The  reliability of MO TFTs have been investigated for years,  mainly focusing on negative-bias-stress-induced instability  [5],   positive-bias-stress-induced   instability   [6],  negative-bias-illumination-stress-induced instability [7] and  positive-bias-illumination-stress-induced instability [7]. For  the output breakdown (OBD) characteristics of MO TFTs,  rare studies have been performed.

In this work, the OBD characteristics of MO TFTs are  symmetrically investigated. Three different kinds of OBD  characteristics are observed, depending on the gate voltage.  The degradation behaviors under OBD voltage stress are  also studied. It is found that device severely degenerates  under OBD stress within very short stress time. After 1 s  OBT stress, the on-current and subthreshold swing ( SS )  respectively degenerates   ${\sim}70\%$   and   ${\sim}430\%$  . The high  electric field at drain side may be responsible. Based on the  experimental results and TCAD simulations, it is inferred  the acceptor-like states in the tail distribution play an  important role during the degradation.

## II.   EXPERIMENTAL  

The schematic diagram of MO TFT is shown in Fig. 1.  In this work, the indium-tin-zinc oxide (ITZO) film is  selected as active layer, which is deposited by co-sputtering  method. The substrate is 4-inch lightly p-type doped Si  wafers with a layer of thick thermal oxide covered. The gate,  source and drain use indium tin oxide (ITO) deposited by  DC sputtering technique. Both gate insulator and passivation  layer are   $\mathrm{SiO}_{2}$  generated by plasma-enhanced chemical  vapor deposition method. After fabrication, an annealing at   $300\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$   for 4 hours was performed on the wafer. The channel  width ( W ) and channel length   $(L)$   are respectively fixed at   $100\,\upmu\mathrm{m}$   and   $10\,\upmu\mathrm{m}$   for MO TFTs under test. The devices are  measured before and after stress by using the  Keysight  B1500A  semiconductor parameter analyzer.

When applying smaller  positive  $V_{g}$  , the  $I_{d s}$   suddenly decreases at the certain point, as  shown in Fig. 5. In this situation, the OBD voltage is around  22 V. When further increasing positive   $V_{g}$   to 15 V, the OBD  voltage increases to  ${\sim}80\;\mathrm{V}$  , as shown in Fig. 6. OBD voltage  decreases with increasing   $V_{g}$  .

## IV. C ONCLUSION 

To investigate the degradation behaviors under OBD  voltage stress, a combination of low   $V_{g s}$   and high  $V_{d s}$   is selected.  From the transfer curve degradation of ITZO TFT under OBD  voltage stress, the OBD stress brings severe device degradation  within very short time. This striking degradation may be  resulted in by high electric field induced by OBD stress. Based  on TCAD simulation, it is inferred that the acceptor-like states  in the tail distribution play an important role in such  OBD-stress-induced degradation.

The OBD characteristics of ITZO TFTs are systematically  investigated. Three kinds of OBD characteristics are observed,  corresponding to the off-state, subthreshold region and on-state
