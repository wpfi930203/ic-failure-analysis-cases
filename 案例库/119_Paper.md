## A single-event irradiation failure analysis test system for high speed digital-to-analog converter   

Yafeng Wei 1,2 ※ , Jing Li 1,2 , Zhou   $\mathrm{Y}\mathrm{u}^{3}$  , Chao Chen 3 , Jun Liu 3 , Xianchao Wen 1 , Jian’an Wang 3   1.Sichuan Institute of Solid-State Circuits, No.23, Xiyong Road, Chongqing city, CO 400060 China    2.University of Electronic Science and Technology of China , No.4, Section 2, North Jianshe Road, Chengdu, Sichuan ， CO 610054 China    3.Science and Technology on Analog Integrated Circuit Laboratory,No.23, Xiyong Road, Chongqing city, CO 400060 China   ※ phone:  $+86$   13658373458; Email:  weiyafeng2004@ 126.com

Abstract- Aerospace devices are vulnerable to heavy ion radiation in  space, and the digital-to-analog (D/A) converters are indispensable  components for aerospace equipment signal transmitter-receiver units.  The research of on-line test technology and test system for  aerospace-class high-speed D/A converters can be used for failure  analysis and test evaluation.In this paper, a single event effect (SEE)  experiment on-line monitoring system is designed, which can easily  and effectively monitor the SEL, SEFI, SEU and SET event of D/A  converter. The experiment was completed by HIRFL and HI-13.  Finally, the result and conclusion are obtained.

Keywords –Failure analysis,Test system,SEL,SEU,SET,SEFI, Soft  breakdown, Digital-to-analog converter.

## I.  INTRODUCTION  

During the orbital operation of the satellite, it is inevitable to  encounter the Earth's radiation belt protons, the Milky Way cosmic  ray particles, and the solar cosmic ray particles. These radiation  high-energy particles generate a large number of electron-hole  pairs when they enter the semiconductor device. These  electron-hole pairs may be present. A short-lived current is formed  by the electric field in the source region of MOSFET. And then a  pulse of the signal or a logic state error or a potential parasitic  effect in the circuit are caused. It will cause a circuit error or even  destroying the device,degraded or functionally disabled.

For the high-speed D/A converter, there are a large number of  digital modules inside. The digital circuit is prone to single-event  upset (SEU) after heavy ion radiation, which causes logic errors.  The analog circuit is subjected to heavy ion radiation damage on  the analog output. The "abnormal glitch", that is, single-event  transient (SET), and the large number of digital registers present in  the circuit are also susceptible to single-event effects, causing local  functional failure, ie, functional interruption (SEFI), if heavy ion  energy is very Strong, D / A converter without special  reinforcement design, it is very prone to fatal errors, in the current  surge, the conversion function is lost, the entire circuit stops  working, that is, single-event blocking (SEL) occurs.

Different from the extensive research abroad, the domestic  research on SEE mainly focuses on the anti-radiation design theory

and method of the circuit, but there are few studies on the SEE  on-line test of high-speed DA converter, especially SEU and SET.  The purpose of this research is to study the single-event effect  mechanism of high-speed D/A converter, study the online test  technology of single-event irradiation test failure analysis of  high-speed D/A converter, and establish an operational online test  for failure analysis. The device is fully tested to assess the  single-event effect risk faced by D/A converter space applications.

In order to accurately establish a test system for failure analysis,  this paper will start with the study of D/A converter structure, study  the single-event effect and its generation mechanism of D/A  converter. Based on this, we will build an online test that can  accurately capture and measure the above effects.

First, the SEE mechanism of high-speed D/A converter is  studied(Fig. 1). The main structure such as internal data interface,  DLL, decoder, SPI, clock, reference unit, bias unit and current  source are divided into three parts according to digital domain,  clock domain and analog domain, respectively, and D/A converter  is respectively performed. The 3D device numerical simulation and single-event  effect modeling are used to analyze SEE mechanism of analog and  digital units of the D/A converter

Secondly, through the research of D/A converter low noise clock  input, high speed SPI interface, multi-state data pattern generate,  high-speed pulse frequency response analysis and capture and  other key technologies to achieve multi-bus instrument  interconnection test system integration design(Fig. 2).

It is found through research that the inherent jitter of the clock  increases the clock noise. If the clock noise is introduced into the  device, it will increase the disturbance of the internal signal  transmission of the device, which will increase the noise floor of  the analog output of the device, which in turn will interfere with the  transient pulse signal. By studying the relationship between clock jitter and noise ,it is  considered that the clock jitter has a great influence on the  signal-to-noise ratio for high-speed D/A converters. And   a  low-jitter clock source is must used to sample small-amplitude  pulse glitch. Therefore, a low jitter clock source module to provide  clock excitation for the online SEE test system is innovatively  designed.

The high-speed D/A converter contains a large number of digital  registers that can accurately measure the flipping of digital cells by  high-speed SPI read back. For example, in the 0.18um process, the  SPI register design of 10000 addresses needs to occupy 1 square  millimeter of chip area, and the calculation rate is 10000. The test  system needs at least 24MHz SPI read/write speed to accurately  measure the number of flips of the device. This project will  develop a high-speed SPI read/write module based on FPGA to  realize the flip test of digital registers.

This paper designs a high-performance data pattern generator  (DPG) based on Virtex6 FPGA, which provides flexible digital  input for D/A converter to generate different analog output. D/A  converter is worked in dynamic mode by applying digital sine  wave for SEL and SEFI tests. Static code values are applied to  work with DC output mode. Different input code values are set  separately for comparison of SET pulse amplitude and pulse width.

In addition, because of the internal structure and working mode  of the oscilloscope, it has a sampling dead time, and the signal  actually captured only accounts for about   $1\%$   of the real signal.  Therefore, we can only use the segmentation storage method to  store the waveform of the sporadic glitch signal continuously.

The oscilloscope has a variety of trigger modes. Generally, the  window is used to trigger the statistics for a period of time, and the  relationship between the trend of the positive and negative pulses  and the code value applied by the D/A converter is observed. The  capture statistics are then performed by glitch or pulse triggering  based on the relationship between the code value and the positive  and negative pulses. Finally, pulse width trigger monitoring is  performed on signals such as the D/A converter data synchronous  clock, and the single-event phenomenon of the clock circuit is  counted.

Based on the above analysis, the maximum number of segments  (frames) can be calculated: we estimate from the fluence rate of   $10000\,/\,\mathrm{cm}^{2}\bullet\mathrm{s},$  , and the area of the single-event radiation-sensitive  area of the D / A converter chip is  $0.9\;\mathrm{mm}^{2}$   . Assume that each ion

can cause a SET effect in the sensitive area of the analog circuit.  By calculation, the maximum probability of “transient glitch” is 90  times/second. It is assumed that each stored waveform is 1 ns long,  with half an hour time required for a single event test to require a  total throughput of 1E7, the result is that the oscilloscope needs to  respond to 162,000 triggers and stores. According to Figure 5, the  oscilloscope should have a memory depth greater than 250MB.

## IV. CONCLUSION  

Through theoretical research and experiment test, a new online  test technology for single event failure analysis was established.  and an operational test scheme is finished. The test results of  Lanzhou HIRFL and Beijing HI-13 accelerator show that the test  technology is correct, the test system is trustworthy, and can be  used for the on-line test of the single-event test failure analysis of  the aerospace-class digital-to-analog converter device and  application verification of space anti-irradiation equipment.
