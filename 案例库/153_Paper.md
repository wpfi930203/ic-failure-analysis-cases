## A Novel MIM sandwich structure of  the SCM probe  for tool inspection   

\* Yu Chen Huang 1 , Yuan Wei Li 1 , Chun Ming Tsai 1 , and Cheng long Pan 2   1 United Microelectronics Co., Ltd.    No. 18, Nake 2 nd  Rd., Tainan Science Park, Sinshih Dist. Tainan City , Taiwan, R.O.C  2 United Semi (Xiamen) Co., Ltd  .No.899 Wan Jia Chun Road, Xiang’an District, Xiamen 361100, P.R.China     Tel 886-6-5054888 ext.13033   Fax: 886-6-5050668      Email:  iris_yc_huang@umc.com

Abstract -   In this article we combine FIB-CVD technology on the  probe and create a novel MIM structure for tool inspection, it  does not require surface preparation process and easy to measure  the p-n junction effectively in order to observe the electronic hole  impurity distribution. We’ve replaced traditional SCM probe tip  by new probe which are consisted of the tungsten layer and oxide  layer as the capacitive dielectric. We solved the problem of the  sample preparation process bring about SCM image quality  instability problem. In addition, the most advantage is the new  MIM probe can achieve the goal of direct measurement on  sample without any sample pretreatment procedure.

Keywords  – SCM probe , FIB-CVD, failure analysis

## I. I NTRODUCTION 

SCM is most conducive to N-P junction inspection tool. It  has been used to determine the 2D dopant profile of wafer  cross-sections for many years.  It is sensitive to carrier density  concentrations from 1015 to 1020 atoms/cm3, with a lateral  resolution of 20–150 nm. SCM  was developed in 1981 earned  its inventors, Gerd Binnig and Heinrich Rohrer (at IBM  Zürich), the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1986. It is based on the  properties of a metal–insulator–semiconductor diode. A  schematic of traditional SCM tip is presented in Figure 1(a) .  Through AC/DC bias voltage applied to semiconductor  structure, and used to induce electronic hole pair produce  accumulation or depletion phenomenon. DC bias dependence  of the capacitance and the differential capacitance,  respectively. Figure 1(b)(c) shows a typical high frequency  capacitance-voltage (C-V) curve for p-type and n-type  semiconductor samples[1].

Although SCM technology has been developed for many  years, it is still not a routine technique for failure analysis and  characterization. One of the underlying problems is that the  SCM data is always not reproducible. The sample preparation  technique is an important influence factor, there is not only the  problem of the surface conditions control (charges, roughness,  & etc.) but also gate dielectrics in MOS transistors the  influence of oxide quality on the trap charge density. An ideal  oxide for SCM should have minimum trap charge [2]. In this  article we combine FIB-CVD technology on the probe to  achieve the purpose of  directly measurement on sample in  order to solve the sample preparation unstable problem.

Traditional SCM probe is the Platinum-Iridium reflective  coating on the backside of the cantilever, the SEM image show  as Fig.2. The MOS structure is consisted of the dielectric layer  which was form by thermal oxidation process. The contact  point between probe and  semiconductor is a MOS structure  show as Figure 3(a), it can be regarded as a system in which the  electrostatic capacitance Cox of the oxide film on the  semiconductor surface and the electrostatic capacitance Cs of  the semiconductor are connected.

But there is a very serious problem about the native oxide  layer quality control which affect the capacitance signal in  traditional SCM probe. We use to adjust the baking method of  the sample heating temperature and time, but these conditions  were bring about a change of oxide thickness. The more  shorter baking time and the lower temperature will reduced the  oxide thickness. When the oxide thickness more thinner and  more lesser voltage through the sample lead to poor image  resolution. [3]. The compactness of  native oxide layer is also  one of the factors, if  there had a trap charge between the native  oxide and Si layer , it could be cause  a change in the C-V  curve.

## IV. C ONCLUSION 

We combine FIB-CVD technology on the New MIM tip  production process. A tungsten layer was imported and the  capacitor layer was added simultaneously in the middle section  of tip, then we could reach our goal of direct measurement on  sample without any sample pretreatment procedure, in order  to  solve the sample preparation unstable problem. In this study,  the Novel MIM  structure probe could be used for SCM   inspection tool  and successfully obtain a better image quality.
